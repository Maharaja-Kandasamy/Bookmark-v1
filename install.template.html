Add to your bookmarks

  1. Show your bookmarks bar with Ctrl + Shift + B.
  2. Drag a dark bookmark button above onto the bar.
  3. Open the matching GSRS screen and click your saved bookmark.

Can't drag? Copy the bookmark link and paste it into a new bookmark's URL.

Both tools run only when you click them. Refreshing the page clears GSRS Visuals; use Kill Bot to close Picker.
