Picker

Use this to perform the mandatory search on the Manual Match screen.

Picker Bookmark v1

Drag the button to your bookmarks bar, or copy its link into a new bookmark’s URL.

How it works →

Get started

  1. Show your bookmarks bar with Ctrl + Shift + B.
  2. Drag the Picker bookmark button onto the bar.
  3. Open a Store or Retail Manual Match screen in GSRS and click your bookmark.
  4. Use the robot’s search controls, then open the results to review candidates.

Already using Picker? Replace the saved bookmark for updates. Use Kill Bot or reload GSRS before launching the new version.

Made for Manual Match

Run banner, address, phone and store-number searches, compare returned candidates, and copy the information you need. Each run reads the current record.

Picker runs when you open its bookmark. Use Kill Bot to stop searches and close it.
