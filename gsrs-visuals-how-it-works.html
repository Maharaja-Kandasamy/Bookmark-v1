How GSRS Visuals works

Each child value is compared with its own parent in the same field. The panel summarizes the current page.

New and Change Records

The same bookmark works on either screen and follows the active tab. On Change Records, SL PHONE NUMBER and PHONE are compared separately, alongside PHONE AREA CODE.

Move it where you need it

Drag the popup’s header anywhere on the screen. It stays within the screen edges when you resize the window. You can also focus the header with Tab and use arrow keys to move it; hold Shift for larger steps.

Your controls

Recheck refreshes the visible comparisons. Show 100 optionally changes the page size and waits for the table to reload. Remove closes Visuals and clears its highlights. Normal table updates are rechecked automatically.

Choose comparisons

Use the checkbox beside each field in the expanded panel to enable or disable its comparison. Disabled fields lose their highlights immediately. Choices apply while Visuals is open. Matching main addresses with different or missing unit details show amber.

Phone comparison

Phone punctuation, a leading US country code and extensions are ignored. Both phone columns compare the last seven digits, preserving zeros. If a parent number contains an area code, it is checked against the child’s separate area-code value. Matching last-seven digits with a different or missing area code are amber; different last-seven digits stay uncolored.

Read the colours

ExactPartialNo match (uncolored)
  • Case, spacing, punctuation, accents and numeric leading zeros are ignored. Common US street abbreviations and state names or codes are standardized.
  • House numbers up to 10 apart are partial matches (amber) when the street matches. A number within a stated door-number range, or overlapping ranges, is also amber. House numbers more than 10 apart and non-overlapping ranges stay uncolored. Addresses sharing the same house number and core street name with an extra business prefix or missing street words or direction are partial matches. Whole-word containment in store or city names and ZIP5 versus ZIP+4 are also partial matches.
  • Other differences, including route numbers, stay uncolored. Blank values on either side are excluded.

Counts cover only the current page’s child values. These deterministic rules are not postal verification or GSRS match scores. No MATCH or REVIEW checkbox is changed. No data is sent or stored. Refreshing the GSRS page clears Visuals.
