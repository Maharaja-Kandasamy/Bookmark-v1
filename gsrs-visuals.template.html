How it works →

Get started

  1. Show your bookmarks bar with Ctrl + Shift + B.
  2. Drag the GSRS Visuals bookmark button onto the bar.
  3. Open New Records in GSRS and click your bookmark.
  4. Review the field highlights and drag the panel header where you need it.

Already using Visuals? Replace the saved bookmark for updates, then reload GSRS before opening it.
