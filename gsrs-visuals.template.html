Move it where you need it

Drag the popup’s header anywhere on the screen. It stays within the screen edges when you resize the window. You can also focus the header with Tab and use arrow keys to move it; hold Shift for larger steps.

Your controls

Recheck refreshes the visible comparisons. Show 100 optionally changes the page size and waits for the table to reload. Remove closes Visuals and clears its highlights. Normal table updates are rechecked automatically.

Read the colours

ExactPartialNo match
  • Case, spacing, punctuation, accents and numeric leading zeros are ignored. Common US street abbreviations and state names or codes are standardized.
  • Addresses differing only in the leading house number, whole-word containment in store or city names, and ZIP5 versus ZIP+4 are partial matches.
  • Other differences, including route or unit numbers, remain red. Blank values on either side are excluded.

Counts cover only the current page’s child values. These deterministic rules are not postal verification or GSRS match scores. No MATCH or REVIEW checkbox is changed. No data is sent or stored. Refreshing the GSRS page clears Visuals.
